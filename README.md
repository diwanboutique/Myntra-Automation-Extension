## DIWAN BOUTIQUE Features

1. **License Activation** — Enter your license key to activate the extension and view your plan duration and expiry countdown.
2. **Extension Updates** — When an update is available, **Download update** appears below the license key in the main popup. Finish your batch, download and extract the ZIP, replace the files in your existing extension folder, then click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions` and refresh the Myntra tab.
3. **QZ Tray Setup** — Open **Pair this PC**, select your local certificate and private key, and test the connection. If prompted for your certificate, select **Remember this decision** and click **Allow**. Choose your label and invoice printers.
4. **Courier Bag Scanning** — Set the code printed on your Myntra courier bag as the **Default Cover ID**, or enable **Scan per order** when bag codes vary.
5. **Picklist Automation** — Create a new picklist or enter an existing Picklist ID to resume it.
6. **Processing Speed** — Choose **Normal**, **Express**, or **Faster**, then click **Resume**.
7. **Batch Printing** — Choose **Print Single** to confirm each order or **Print All** to process the full picklist.
8. **Packet-wise Printing** — Print each packet’s label and invoice together. Separate shipments stay separate, even for the same customer.
9. **PDF Saving** — Create a folder on your PC and select it under **PDF Save Settings → Choose / authorize folder**. Choose **Allow on every visit** if offered. Enable **Save a PDF while printing** for paper and PDF, or select **Save as PDF** in both printer menus for PDF only. Each packet’s label and invoice are saved together as `YYYY-MM-DD/PacketID.pdf`.
10. **Activity Log** — Follow QC, Ready to Ship, print progress, and errors for each packet.
11. **Excel Export** — Download order and packet IDs, available tracking numbers, prices, customer delivery details, and print status in one Excel report.
